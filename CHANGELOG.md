# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- **Choose what's analysed first.** Long-press games in Games, pick as many as you like and tap *Analyse first*: they're analysed in the order you picked them, before everything else. *All waiting* picks every unanalysed game that matches the current filters.
- **Analysis queue screen** (from Games, Home or an unanalysed game): the game being analysed now, your picks with estimated ready times (drag to reorder, or use each row's menu), and what comes next automatically.
- **Redesigned review screen.** Moves and Report tabs; the coach card sits right under the board; large labelled controls; *Mistake* jumps between your errors; swipe the board to step through moves.
- **Show the follow-up.** *Show best line* plays the engine's better continuation on the board by itself, and *After …* plays how the game would go on after the move you made. Pause, step, replay or go back to the game at any point.
- *Analyse next* on an unanalysed game puts it at the front of your picks and shows its place in the queue.
